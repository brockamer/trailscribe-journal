We ate on the bluff as the sun dropped into the water. The air stayed warm, barely a breeze off the ocean. Zuma stretched out below us, emptying of swimmers, turning gold. Nobody needed to say much. The kind of evening that earns its place in memory without trying. We stayed until the horizon swallowed the last of it.
