Stars press close to shore  
salt air holds the cooling dark  
waves count out the hours

Warm enough to sit outside without a layer. The offshore breeze barely moves the grass. Sky is clean all the way to the horizon — no marine layer tonight, which is rare this time of year. The Pacific makes its usual low noise in the background, steady and indifferent. Hard to ask for a better night out here. Turned in late, reluctantly.
