Twenty-four ounces of coffee in and I'm planning the move out. Not packing yet, just thinking it through: what comes with me, what waits, what goes. A storage locker keeps coming up as the answer for the in-between stuff. Still weighing it. The coffee is doing its job. The plan is slower to come together, but it's starting to take shape.
