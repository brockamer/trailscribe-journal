Before the sun climbs  
a cursor blinks, code takes shape  
the day waits outside

Early morning, indoors. The work today is AI coding, not miles. A screen, a prompt, a reply, then another round of refining. Outside the light comes up slowly while the problem on the screen takes its own slow shape. Even off the trail there is a kind of route-finding: test a line, see where it goes, back up, try again. The day outside can wait a little longer.
