Legs gone soft as sand  
the tide pulls harder than me  
still I keep running

Afternoon on the sand at Malibu, 96 and clear, barely a breeze off the water. Feeling weak. The tide's running strong, stronger than me right now. Kept running anyway. Note to self: in heat like this, weak legs mean stop, drink water, find shade, and stay clear of the surf line. The ocean will still be here when I've cooled down.
