Late afternoon on the coast road. Traffic moves in waves, stopping and starting through a construction zone, while the ocean keeps its own steady rhythm nearby. 95 degrees under a clear sky, barely any breeze to break the heat. Engines idle, cones line the shoulder, and the line inches forward a few car lengths at a time. The water carries on, unbothered by any of it.
