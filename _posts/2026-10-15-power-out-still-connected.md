Wind takes down the lines  
two SIM cards hold the signal  
batteries hum on

High winds knocked the power out, but it hardly matters. My devices all run on batteries, and I carry my own Internet with me wherever I go: a GL.iNet travel router and SIM cards from two different providers. If one network falters, the other picks up the slack. The grid can go down, and I stay online.
