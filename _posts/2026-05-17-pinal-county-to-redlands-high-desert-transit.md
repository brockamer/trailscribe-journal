Departed Pinal County under a hard Arizona sun, temperature already sitting at 89°F by the time the odometer began turning. The sky was cloudless and the wind ran at a steady 16 mph out of the southwest, enough to push a little lateral drift on the open stretches but not enough to matter at highway pace. The land here is flat in the way that only the Sonoran Desert manages — not featureless, but relentlessly horizontal, broken by saguaro stands and the occasional dry wash cutting beneath the road grade.

The route ran point-to-point, 611.98 kilometers from start to finish, completed in 517 minutes — just under eight and a half hours wheels-to-wheels. Average speed held at 91.1 km/h, which is honest highway travel accounting for any slowdowns through towns and construction corridors. The p95 speed of 132.2 km/h tells the story of the open desert and interstate stretches where traffic thinned and the road simply invited speed.

Elevation gain across the full route totaled 2,336 meters — modest for the distance, but the profile was not flat. The climb out of the lower Sonoran basin, up through the transition zone near the California border, registered in the legs even from a driver's seat: the engine note changed, the desert scrub shifted from saguaro and palo verde toward joshua tree and creosote, and the air temperature dropped a few degrees as the road lifted toward the high Mojave margin.

The California border crossing brought a different quality of light — lower angle, more diffuse, the kind of afternoon illumination that turns the San Bernardino Mountains into a blue-gray wall on the western horizon. The final approach into Redlands followed the descent off the high desert floor, the Inland Empire spreading out below in haze and grid, the San Gorgonio massif holding snow on its upper flanks even in this heat.

Arrival in Redlands closed the session. The drive covered nearly the full width of the Sonoran and Mojave transition in a single sustained push, the metrics reflecting a clean, uninterrupted run across one of the more austere stretches of the American Southwest.
