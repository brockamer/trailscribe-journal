gray backs breach and roll  
Pacific holds its cold breath  
we forget to speak

Followed the trail up from the Zuma lot in mild air, clouds keeping the glare soft. From the ridge the ocean opened wide — and there they were. Spouts first, then the slow dark roll of backs. Hard to say how many. Two, maybe three. Nobody on the trail said much. You just stop and watch until they're gone, and then you keep watching anyway.
