Midday, not a cloud  
the whole world laid out below  
I just stand and stare

Holy shit, this view. Seriously. Half past eleven and I just had to stop and take it in. Seventy degrees, barely a breeze, not a single cloud, the kind of clear where everything seems closer than it is. The Ventura County hills roll off in every direction and I'm standing here with my jaw hanging open. Nothing better to write than the first thing I said. Holy shit.
