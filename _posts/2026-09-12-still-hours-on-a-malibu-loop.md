Long stops in the field are where the actual observation happens — where you notice the way the light shifts across the chaparral, where a lizard decides you are furniture and goes about its business, where the breeze picks up for thirty seconds and then settles back to its baseline 8 mph and you feel the difference.

Malibu in clear weather at 76°F does not demand movement. It rewards patience. The loop — such as it was — closed where it opened, the elevation returned to where it started, and 258 minutes passed in the way that good field time passes: without urgency and without much to show for it except the having been there.
