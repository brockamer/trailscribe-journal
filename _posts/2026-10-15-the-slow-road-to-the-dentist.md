Brake lights in a line  
the slow road to the dentist  
no rush to arrive

Not the backcountry today. Traffic on the way to the dentist, a long crawl of stopped cars. There are worse ways to delay an appointment like that. Logging it anyway: even the paved routes have their slow passages, and some destinations make the waiting feel less like a hardship.
