salt air fills the lungs  
feet find rhythm on dry trail  
the coast holds me still

Out on the Zuma ridge, just moving. No pace, no watch — just the long slow distance kind of day where the miles stack up quiet. Partly cloudy kept the sun honest. Six miles of chaparral and the smell of sage and ocean mixing together in a way that's hard to explain. Legs loose, breathing easy. The Pacific sat out there the whole time, barely moving. Neither did I, really. Just kept going.
