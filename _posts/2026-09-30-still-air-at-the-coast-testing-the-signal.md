Warm window light falls  
pen scratches across the page  
the coast waits outside

Inside, the air sits at sixty-nine and easy. A light wind moves somewhere beyond the glass. Nothing to report yet — just checking that the notes will carry. Malibu holds its breath on clear days like this, the kind where the hills go amber and the Pacific flattens out like hammered tin. When there is something worth writing, I will be ready.
