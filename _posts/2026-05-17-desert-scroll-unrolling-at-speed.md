spined arms blur and streak
joshua trees lean and watch
the road takes me through

Driving east through the park, the desert does its usual trick of looking still until you're moving through it. Cholla clusters catch the afternoon light from the passenger window, each needle bright as wire. The joshua trees stand at odd angles like they're mid-conversation, indifferent to the Prius humming past. Eighty-six degrees and not a cloud to diffuse the glare. The basin opens wide between the ridgelines.
